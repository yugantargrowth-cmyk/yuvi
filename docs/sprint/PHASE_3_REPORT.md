## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 3 implements the **Needs-Based Proposal Engine** and **Truth Guardrail System** for Yugantar Growth. Under this engine, proposals are never generic templates; they are generated by a deterministic `needs-analysis` skill that selects the top 3 commercial revenue leaks directly linked to discrete `VERIFIED` and `OBSERVED` Evidence IDs. 

The output produces three synchronized client-facing assets:
1. **1-Page Printable A4 Proposal**: Built to fit strictly onto one printable sheet (10mm margins, A4 portrait) formatted with the authentic Yugantar Growth brand palette: Deep Black (`#080808`), Electric Orange (`#e8520a`), and Warm Cream (`#f7f5f0`). It includes the 3 diagnosed leaks with Evidence IDs, prescribed deliverables, transparent pricing, GST toggle, an advance payment gate, and instant UPI payment routing to `shlokpandya@oksbi`.
2. **Hinglish WhatsApp Pitch**: A conversational, non-pushy founder pitch strictly constrained to **<= 6 lines** mentioning 1 sharp verified observation and a low-friction roadmap conversation ask.
3. **Executive Cold Email**: An evidence-backed email citing 2 verified facts and a 5-minute call invitation.

## 2. Key Deliverables

### A. `needs-analysis` Skill (`lib/sales/needsAnalysisEngine.ts`)
- **Evidence-to-Leak Mapper:** Filters evidence through truth guardrails, selecting the top 3 commercial revenue leaks.
- **Catalog Calibrator:** Automatically prescribes packages from the standardized Yugantar Service Catalog:
  - `web_sprint_48h` (Fast Web Dev & WhatsApp Concierge, ₹25,000, 100% advance gate, 48h turnaround).
  - `outbound_pipeline` (Direct B2B Outbound Acquisition & ICP Funnel, ₹45,000, 50% advance).
  - `growth_os_retainer` (Turnkey Growth Operating System Retainer, ₹1,20,000/mo, 100% advance).
- **Outbound Guardrail Enforcement:** Throws an explicit error if a lead has fewer than 2 `VERIFIED` or `OBSERVED` evidence items, strictly preventing ungrounded pitches from reaching prospects.

### B. `proposal-compose` Skill (`lib/sales/proposalComposer.ts`)
- **1-Page Printable A4 HTML/CSS Generator:** Strict CSS `@page { size: A4 portrait; margin: 10mm; }` styling ensures no overflow onto page 2.
- **WhatsApp Pitch Formatter with Line Count Guardrail:** `validateWhatsAppLineCount()` validates compliance with the `<= 6 lines` constraint.
- **GST Configuration:** Supports `gstEnabled: false` (0% tax for unregistered/exempt tier) and `gstEnabled: true` (18% tax).

### C. 3-Tab Proposal Document & Pitch Viewer in `ProposalsView.tsx`
- **1-Page Printable A4 Tab:** Renders the branded proposal with diagnosed leaks, deliverables, pricing, advance terms, UPI gateway, and a "Print / Save as PDF" button.
- **WhatsApp Pitch Tab:** Displays the Hinglish pitch, compliance badge (`Line Count: 6/6`), 1-click Copy button, and direct `wa.me` launch link.
- **Cold Email Tab:** Formatted subject and body with Copy buttons.
- **Auto-Compose Button in Create Modal:** "Auto-Analyze Needs & Prescribe Catalog Package" analyzes the selected lead and populates deliverables, pricing, and terms with one click.
